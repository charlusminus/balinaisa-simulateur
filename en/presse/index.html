Press release.

La Teste-de-Buch, Arcachon Bay, September 2026.

Balinaisa.ai reinvents the way spaces are imagined

Revealing everything an interior or an outdoor space could be, from a single photograph: that is the wager behind Balinaisa.ai, the new Artificial Intelligence devoted to interior design and layout.

Timed with the Mode&Objet trade fair and Paris Design Week, Balinaisa, a French furniture and decoration house specialising in exceptional teak, is unveiling Balinaisa.ai, its new Artificial Intelligence tool for the design and layout of private and professional spaces: living room, dining room, terrace, garden, restaurant, hotel.

The principle is simple: photograph your space, indoors or out. One minute later, Balinaisa.ai sends back an image of it refurnished, together with a quote for the pieces in it, priced on the 97 real references in the company catalogue, from the garden armchair to the pendant light. Free, no account needed, at balinaisa.ai.

Photo, plan, quote

One photo, a budget if you have one, an email address. Within a minute the rendering and the quote arrive, in the same framing and from the same angle as the original photo.

The pricing is not a per-category estimate: every piece of furniture shown is a reference from the Balinaisa catalogue, at its real price before tax. 97 references, 17 families, from the garden armchair to the pendant light. Two proposals arrive, the complete scene as it appears in the image and the selection that fits the stated budget, with the gap between them. The quote is an estimate.

The technology, and what happens to your photos

An automation pipeline built for Balinaisa with Paktiz, its technical partner, running on latest-generation models. The difference is made by the catalogue: for each selected product the image generator receives the reference photo of the piece and its description, which forces it to reproduce furniture that exists instead of inventing some.

The photo you upload and the rendering are used only to produce the simulation and follow up on the project. They are never passed on to third parties, they are deleted automatically after 36 months, and the simulator uses no advertising cookie and no cross-site tracker. Full policy at balinaisa.ai/privacy-policy.html, deletion on request from contact@balinaisa.com.

Dominique Raynal's eye

Behind Balinaisa.ai there is not only a technology. There is, above all, an eye. That of Dominique Raynal, a maker and specialist of exceptional teak furniture, whose experience, knowledge of the material, sense of proportion and way of imagining the relationship between furniture and architecture form the creative DNA of the Balinaisa house. Artificial Intelligence does not replace that eye: it extends it, and makes an idea immediately visible and costed.

« I wanted to pair my expertise with Artificial Intelligence, to offer a more immediate, visual and personal way of imagining a space. I shaped Balinaisa.ai as an extension of my craft. Today it composes for you, in your own home, as if I were pushing your door open beside you. » Dominique Raynal, chairman and chief executive of Balinaisa

For private clients and professionals alike

Balinaisa.ai speaks naturally to private customers wanting to rethink their interior or their outdoor spaces. But the professional uses are just as many. Interior architects, decorators, designers, landscape designers, hoteliers, restaurateurs and property or fit-out professionals can use the tool to speed up the projection of a scheme and make conversations with their own clients easier.

For Balinaisa, then, Artificial Intelligence does not replace the designer's work: it shortens the distance between an idea, seeing it, and knowing what it costs.
