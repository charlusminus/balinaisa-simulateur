Balinaisa launches an AI tool that turns a photo of a room into a costed interior plan

Photograph your living room, your terrace or your restaurant floor. One minute later, Balinaisa.ai sends back an image of your space refurnished, together with a quote for the furniture in it, priced on the 98 real references in the company catalogue. Free, no account needed, at balinaisa.ai.

La Teste-de-Buch, September 2026.

Photo, plan, quote

One photo, a budget if you have one, an email address. Within a minute the rendering and the quote arrive, in the same framing and from the same angle as the original photo.

The pricing is not a per-category estimate: every piece of furniture shown is a reference from the Balinaisa catalogue, at its real price before tax. 98 references, 17 families, from the garden armchair to the pendant light. Two proposals arrive, the complete scene as it appears in the image and the selection that fits the stated budget, with the gap between them. The quote is an estimate.

The technology, and what happens to your photos

An automation pipeline built for Balinaisa with Paktiz, its technical partner, running on latest-generation models. The difference is made by the catalogue: for each selected product the image generator receives the reference photo of the piece and its description, which forces it to reproduce furniture that exists instead of inventing some.

The photo you upload and the rendering are used only to produce the simulation and follow up on the project. They are never passed on to third parties, they are deleted automatically after 36 months, and the simulator uses no advertising cookie and no cross-site tracker. Full policy at balinaisa.ai/privacy-policy.html, deletion on request from contact@balinaisa.com.

Dominique Raynal's eye

Dominique Raynal designs teak furniture and has it made between France and Indonesia. It is his knowledge of the material, and his way of placing a piece within a volume, that the tool applies at every simulation.

Today it composes for you, in your own home, as if I were pushing your door open beside you. » Dominique Raynal, chief executive of Balinaisa
